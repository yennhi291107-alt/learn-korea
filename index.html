📚 What might a short study session look like?

Choose one small goal, put it into practice, and review anything that still feels tricky.

① Warm-up · 5 min

Review five familiar words with quick questions and short examples.

② Focus · 10 min

Pick one grammar pattern or vocabulary theme, such as ordering food or shopping.

③ Practice · 5 min

Role-play a conversation, get feedback, and try saying it again more naturally.
